# Acoustic Detection of Cracks and Delamination in Multilayer Ceramic Capacitors

- **Venue:** IEEE — conference (2017, IEEE Xplore doc 8062419) and journal (2018, doc 8481367).
- **URLs:** https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8062419/ · https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8481367/
- **ResearchGate:** https://www.researchgate.net/publication/328088246
- **Type:** T3 peer-reviewed paper. Captured 2026-06-24 from web research (abstract + secondary summaries; full text not held).

## Key claims (as captured)
- Demonstrates **fast, non-destructive acoustic screening** of MLCCs (brittle dielectric → prone to mechanical damage).
- **Soldered 2220-size MLCCs** subjected to **AC voltage frequency sweeps**, exciting mechanical vibration (electrostriction/piezo response of BaTiO₃).
- **Acoustic response** measured **before and after** the test board was subjected to **severe bending**.
- Cracks and delaminations from bending induce **characteristic changes in the acoustic response** (shifts/new features in the spectrum).
- A **support-vector-machine (SVM) classifier** trained on the acoustic responses **successfully detected damaged capacitors**.

## Notes
- Distinct from C-SAM (scanning *acoustic microscopy*): this is an **electromechanical-resonance / emitted-sound** method using the part's own singing (cf. mlcc-acoustic-noise), not an immersion acoustic-microscope scan.
- An in-circuit / functional screen — complementary to imaging FA (X-ray, C-SAM, DPA).
- Related newer work: "Detection of microdefects in MLCCs using the instantaneous frequency in the electromechanical response" (ResearchGate 387108132, 2024).
