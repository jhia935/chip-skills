# Detection of cracks in multilayer ceramic capacitors by X-ray imaging

- **Authors:** C. Andersson, J. Ingman, et al.
- **Venue:** *Microelectronics Reliability* (Elsevier), 2016. DOI via S0026271416302542.
- **URL:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0026271416302542

- **Type:** T3 peer-reviewed paper. Captured 2026-06-24 from web research (abstract + secondary summaries; full text not held).

## Key claims (as captured)
- 300 MLCCs were investigated by **2D and 3D X-ray imaging** after **bending to varying levels of strain**; cross-section analysis was done to verify the findings.
- MLCCs were mounted in **different orientations relative to the bending direction** to study detectability of bend-induced (flex) cracks.
- X-ray can reveal cracks in many cases, but **tight (closed) flex cracks are difficult** to detect by conventional 2D X-ray — 3D CT improves localization.
- Detection relies on **density contrast**: a crack/void must open enough to create attenuation contrast against the surrounding dielectric.
- Cross-sectioning (DPA) was the ground-truth verification method.

## Open questions / notes
