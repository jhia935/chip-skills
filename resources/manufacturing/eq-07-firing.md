## Equipment structure
- **Setters (firing tooling).** Murata calls them "dish-shaped jigs used when loading products into furnaces"; product placement on the tooling is "a critical factor for improving productivity and preventing defects and variations"; automated handling moves products to designated positions; "millions of products in a single heat treatment cycle" [murata-ht-web]. Conventional MLCC setters are solid zirconia, or refractory (alumina or alumina-silica/mullite) bases with thermally sprayed zirconia [us6461156][krosaki-platect-int]. Zirconia is used because it does "not readily" react with ceramic capacitors, but it is heavy, breaks and deforms [krosaki-platect-int]. Krosaki's plasma-sprayed zirconia-on-alumina-silica setter claims ~40 % of the global MLCC-firing setter market (vendor claim) and lighter setters let more setters be loaded per kiln floor load [krosaki-platect-web][krosaki-platect-int].
- **Setter stacking.** Tray-shaped setters "can be used in a stacked form" so many components fire at once; loading more parts per setter makes temperature and atmosphere-gas uniformity harder [us6461156]. Piezo examples: setters stacked in tiers on columnar zirconia spacers of the same material, an empty setter on top; dense Y2O3-stabilized ZrO2 setter 100 x 100 x 1.5 mm, porosity <3 % [jp2005170769][jp5742293]. Setters/cover plates need some porosity so binder gas escapes; thin green parts may need a lightweight cover plate [us12623968].
- **Anti-stick powder.** "powdered ceramics (Al2O3, ZrO2, etc.) have been applied to furnace fixtures either as dry powders or aqueous washes" to prevent sticking; the patent says this gives poor yields/contamination [us12623968]. Not MLCC-specific; no MLCC source found.
- **Chip-to-setter loading problem.** Cut chips are peeled from the resin sheet and placed on a firing setter; adhesive residue makes chips stick together during alignment on the setter [jps6129114].
- **Debinding, stage 1 (first pre-firing).** Box-type furnace, "low-temperature, slow binder removal", stainless chamber, automatic pressure control; atmosphere may be N2, H2 or water vapor [hengli-3dincites-2026]. General ceramic debinding furnace: decomposition products carried by the purge gas into a water-cooled tar trap, then an exhaust-gas combustor oxidizes VOCs; air or N2 at 0.5-5 L/min (lab unit) [orist-debind-2021]. Superheated-steam low-O2 inert-gas ovens are marketed for MLCC/LTCC debinding (snippet, unverified) [jtekt-allfit-snip].
- **Debinding, stage 2 (second pre-firing).** Continuous belt furnace removes residual organics at a higher temperature; listed features: FEC heating panels, temperature control modules, O2 and H2 monitoring modules, automatic humidification, furnace pressure monitoring, automatic belt tracking, automatic belt tensioning [hengli-3dincites-2026]. Ni oxidation during secondary burn-out depends on H2 content, humidification temperature and furnace temperature (saturated-steam model) [weirong-2025]. Mesh-belt muffle furnaces are marketed for MLCC (snippet, unverified) [jtekt-allfit-snip].
- **Co-firing kiln, pusher type.** Muffle (flame-isolated) chamber, multi-point heating with closed-loop control at each point, multiple gas inlets for zone-by-zone atmosphere; loading on pusher plates or setters/saggers; a long pusher can combine secondary burnout and sintering [hengli-3dincites-2026]. General pusher kiln: works conveyed on base plates by an oil-pressure (hydraulic) pusher; gas supply/exhaust holes with individually adjustable valves; vacuum-substitution entry cuts N2 use by up to 75 %; transfer-speed precision +-1 % (std +-2 %); max 1600 C; atmospheres incl. N2, N2+H2, H2 [noritake-pusher-web]. BTU paper on atmosphere separation and closed-loop O2 control in a BME MLCC pusher furnace (title/summary only) [btu-carts-2001].
- **Co-firing kiln, roller hearth type (RHK).** RHK is "mainly used" for MLCC sintering because of productivity; typical length about 17.5 m (snippet, unverified) [us12729914-bg]. RHK suits higher throughput and shorter cycles, uses rollers, trays and saggars, and sometimes needs the second pre-firing done first [hengli-3dincites-2026]. General RHK: ceramic rollers, radiant heating from all directions, spring drive for high-speed transfer without meandering, +-2 C distribution [noritake-rhk-web]. MLCC RHK chamber study: 24 chambers through preheating, firing and cooling with no partitions between chambers; firing section = 4 chambers; each chamber 275 mm long; N2 enters through inlets from bottom and leaves at top to carry off fumes (3 inlets, 1 outlet chosen for laminar flow); lower heating pipes (2) under and upper heating pipes (2) over the transfer plane [lee-2013-rhk-kais].
- **Heating elements.** MoSi2 elements for >1250 C; a grade intended for N2 above 1250 C because standard MoSi2 nitrides in N2; roof-mounted horizontal installation [kanthal-mlcc-web].
- **Atmosphere system.** Humidified N2/H2 for firing; N2 or mixed gas humidified with a wetter at water temperature about 5-75 C (example 35 C) [us6185087]. Firing gas 0.1-0.5 % H2 / balance N2 (H2O/H2/N2); oxygen-sensor EMF at 850 C: 680 mV for 0.1 % H2, 760 mV for 0.5 % H2 [us12374492]. "Automatic oxygen and dew point control", zone gas blending [hengli-3dincites-2026]. H2O vaporizers and pressure monitors called indispensable; gases listed Air, N2, O2, H2O; MFC, liquid MFC, vaporizers [horiba-cc-web]. Sub-ppm O2 measured directly in the oven in forming gas [csensotec-cc-web].
- **Re-oxidation.** May run in the same furnace or separately [us6185087]; RHK named as a possible re-ox furnace, no detail [hengli-3dincites-2026].

## Step sequence
1. Place cut green chips on setters (zirconia surface); chips peeled from carrier and aligned [jps6129114][murata-ht-web].
2. Stack setters in tiers with spacers; top cover setter (piezo example) [us6461156][jp2005170769].
3. First binder burnout, box furnace, low T, slow [hengli-3dincites-2026]; conditions in patents: air, 5-300 C/h, hold 200-400 C for 0.5-24 h (example 10 C/h to 280 C) [us6185087]; 230 C x 60 h in air [us8953300].
4. Second binder burnout, continuous belt furnace, higher T, humidified H2-containing atmosphere [hengli-3dincites-2026][weirong-2025] — or integrated into a long pusher kiln [hengli-3dincites-2026].
5. Kiln entry: pusher plates pushed hydraulically (pusher) or trays on rollers (RHK) [noritake-pusher-web][noritake-rhk-web][hengli-3dincites-2026].
6. Preheat/atmosphere change -> peak co-firing in humidified N2/H2 -> controlled cooling [us6185087].
7. Re-oxidation: continuous mode = after firing, lower to annealing T and change atmosphere without cooling; separate mode = cool, then reheat in dry or humid N2 [us6185087].
8. Cool and unload; chips go on to barrel chamfering / termination (sequence only; no unloading source found).
