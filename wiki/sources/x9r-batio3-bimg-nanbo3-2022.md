# 2022 — Enhanced Thermal Stability in BaTiO₃–BiMg₁/₂Ti₁/₂O₃–NaNbO₃ Ceramics for X9R-MLCC Applications

**Source**: (2022). **Enhanced Thermal Stability in Dielectric Properties of NaNbO₃-Modified BaTiO₃-BiMg₁/₂Ti₁/₂O₃ Ceramics for X9R-MLCC Applications**. *Crystals*, **12** (2), 141.

## Summary

A demonstration of how **multi-end-member ternary perovskite formulations** are used to hit the **[[x8r-x8l-x9r-high-temperature-dielectrics|X9R envelope]]** (±15 % over −55…+200 °C). The composition `0.5BaTiO₃ – (0.5 − x)BiMg₁/₂Ti₁/₂O₃ – xNaNbO₃` (abbreviated **BT-BMT-NN**) achieves X9R compliance at x = 0.25, with:

- **ε_r ≈ 630 ± 15 %** across −70 to +220 °C (slightly exceeds the −55 to +200 °C X9R requirement)
- **tan δ < 0.025** across −57 to +350 °C
- **Recoverable energy density ~ 0.55 J/cm³**

This composition is representative of the modern Bi-based X8R/X9R lineup — three end-members combining as **peak-shifters and broadeners**.

## Role of each end-member

| End-member | Role | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **BaTiO₃** (50 %) | Provides bulk ferroelectric ε_r; standard Class II base | Tetragonal ferroelectric T_C ~125 °C |
| **BiMg₁/₂Ti₁/₂O₃** (BMT) | Peak-shifter to high T | Bi³⁺ lone-pair drives local strain ([[bi-lone-pair-doping]]); Mg½Ti½ replaces Ti to balance charge |
| **NaNbO₃** (NN) | Peak-broadener and relaxor inducer | Random Na/Nb cation disorder → [[polar-nanoregions-pnr|PNR microstructure]]; broadens dielectric peak |

As NaNbO₃ content `x` increases:
- ε_r at room temperature **drops** from 850 → 564 (more disorder → less single-domain alignment).
- Temperature stability **improves** dramatically (broader peak).
- The X9R sweet spot is at x = 0.25 where the trade-off between ε_r magnitude and thermal stability is optimized.

## Implications for class-specific T_C engineering

This paper is a representative example of **the general X9R / X8R approach**: rather than relying on a single peak-shifter, use **two or three** simultaneously:

1. **Peak-shifter** — push T_C upward (here: BiMg₁/₂Ti₁/₂O₃)
2. **Peak-broadener** — spread the dielectric peak (here: NaNbO₃)
3. **Bulk dielectric provider** — BaTiO₃ provides the ε_r magnitude

Similar ternary systems used in published X8R / X9R formulations:
- `BT–BiScO₃–KNbO₃` (BiScO₃ shifts T_C; KNbO₃ adds disorder)
- `BT–BMT–CaZrO₃` (CaZrO₃ provides internal-stress shifter)
- `Bi₀.₅Na₀.₅TiO₃–NaNbO₃–CaZrO₃` (lead-free NBT-based)
- `(K₀.₈Na₀.₂)NbO₃–BT` (Lead-free KNN-BT)

All share the principle: **at least two end-members beyond BaTiO₃**, balancing shift and broadening.
