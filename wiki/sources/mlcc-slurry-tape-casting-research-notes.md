# MLCC Slurry & Tape-Casting Research Notes

**Type:** compiled research notes from a web survey (2026-11-10). They draw on **12 distinct documents**: mostly T4 patents (SEMCO US7229035, TDK US7632369, Murata US5520994, Lintec US20150086749, Nan Ya US9932452, Steinmueller US6682598), one T3 journal paper (Yang et al. 2026, *J. Phys. Conf. Ser.* 3175), one T3 simulation (Nakata/Kyocera 2021, arXiv), one T3 abstract (Karas 1988), one T3 cover-layer paper (Park 2023), and T5/T6 overviews (Fraunhofer IKTS, IEEE TechNav, Wikipedia citing Mistler & Twiname 2000).

## Key claims
- Fine-bead deagglomeration (0.2–0.3 mm ZrO₂, 6–10 m/s) followed by 10–25 kpsi high-pressure dispersion and 1–2 µm filtration takes the PSD from D90 1.01 µm to D90 0.447 µm (SEMCO).
- For 150 nm BaTiO₃ the optimum is 2.0 wt% dispersant, 20 h milling, and 12 wt% PVB with a 7:3 high:medium molecular-weight blend. Target green-tape properties are tensile ≥15 MPa and elongation 15–25% (Yang 2026).
- MLCC green sheets are claimed at 0.5–30 µm, with 0.5–2 µm as the ultrathin focus. The release layer needs Ra ≤8 nm, and peel static must stay at about 1–1.6 kV or below (TDK, Lintec, Nan Ya).
- TDK example: 180 mPa·s slurry, wire bar at 50 m/min, dry 60–70 °C for 2 min, dry thickness 5–25% of wet.

## Conflicts / gaps
- Casting viscosity: 180 mPa·s (TDK) vs 400–700 cP (Yang). The coating method differs.
- Thickness floor: "5 µm" (textbook) vs 0.5–2 µm (MLCC patents). The difference is the era of the source.
- **No Tier-1 vendor process data.** There is no thickness-uniformity tolerance, and no production slot-die vs doctor-blade data.
