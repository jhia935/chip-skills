# Penn State University

The Pennsylvania State University. Home to the **Center for Dielectrics and Piezoelectrics (CDP)** — a primary US academic hub for ferroelectric ceramics and MLCC research. Both [[susan-trolier-mckinstry]] and (formerly) [[shashank-priya]] are/were on the faculty.

Penn State's CDP runs industry-sponsored research consortia with all the major MLCC vendors and is the academic source of much of the published [[core-shell-batio3|core-shell BaTiO₃]] and [[rayleigh-analysis|Rayleigh-analysis]] literature on Class II dielectrics. Clive Randall (co-inventor on [[us-7670981-kemet-c0g-bme|KEMET C0G BME patents]] and pioneer of [[cold-sintering]]) is also on the CDP faculty.
