# Susan Trolier-McKinstry

Professor of Ceramic Science and Engineering at [[penn-state|Penn State University]] and one of the most-cited researchers in ferroelectric thin films and dielectric materials. Director of the Penn State Center for Dielectrics and Piezoelectrics.

Past President of the American Ceramic Society and the IEEE Ferroelectrics, UFFC.

## Relevance to this wiki

Senior author on [[fujii-trolier-mckinstry-2023-batio3-nonlinearity|Fujii & Trolier-McKinstry 2023]], which provides the temperature-resolved [[rayleigh-analysis|Rayleigh decomposition]] of ε_r in BaTiO₃ that underpins the chip-sim's [[tcc-dc-bias-interaction|TCC × DC-bias coupling model]].

Long-standing program at Penn State on ferroelectric thin films, MEMS, piezo-MEMS, energy-storage capacitors — sits at the intersection of [[batio3]] fundamental science and device engineering. The Penn State group's body of work is a primary academic counterweight to vendor-internal MLCC R&D.
