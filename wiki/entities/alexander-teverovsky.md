# Alexander Teverovsky

NASA Goddard / [[nasa-nepp|NEPP]] reliability researcher (Jacobs/SSAI at GSFC), the most prolific author of NASA's MLCC characterization corpus — rating/derating, leakage & absorption currents, breakdown voltage, cracking, and mechanical (fracture-toughness / MOR) studies. His work supplies much of the design-rule basis behind the GSFC space specs ([[gsfc-mlcc-space-specs]]) and the [[dielectric-layer-thickness-floor|dielectric-thickness]] and [[mlcc-margin-cover-design-rules|margin]] guidance.
