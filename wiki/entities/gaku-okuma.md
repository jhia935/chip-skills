# Gaku Okuma

Materials researcher working on **sintering microstructure of MLCCs** via synchrotron X-ray nano-CT, affiliated with [[tdk]] and the [[fumihiro-wakai|Wakai]] group at Tokyo Institute of Technology. First author of [[okuma-2021-mlcc-electrode-nanoct|Okuma et al. 2021 (Acta Materialia 206, 116605)]], a 3-D nano-CT study of Ni inner-electrode evolution during co-firing that documents in-plane shrinkage anisotropy (tied to tape-casting direction) and sub-µm electrode discontinuity.
