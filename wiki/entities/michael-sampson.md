# Michael J. Sampson

NASA Goddard Space Flight Center parts engineer, co-lead of the [[nasa-nepp|NEPP]] capacitor-reliability tasks and long-time GSFC EEE-parts authority. Co-author with [[donhang-liu]] of the [[nasa-gsfc-liu-construction-database-2011|2011 BME MLCC construction/DPA evaluation]] that underpins the NASA space-level BME qualification effort ([[gsfc-mlcc-space-specs|S-311-P-838]]).
