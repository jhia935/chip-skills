# Sam Ben-Yaakov

Professor emeritus at [[ben-gurion-university|Ben-Gurion University of the Negev]] (Beer-Sheva, Israel). Long-running power-electronics and capacitor-modeling researcher; widely cited for analytic treatments of non-linear capacitors and resonant-converter design.

Author of [[ben-yaakov-2025-nonlinear-capacitance-definition|"Capacitance Definition of Non-Linear Voltage-Dependent Capacitors" (2025)]] — the EPCI article laying out the three distinct capacitance definitions for ferroelectric MLCCs.
