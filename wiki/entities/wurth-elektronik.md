# Würth Elektronik

German passive-components and connector manufacturer, headquartered in Waldenburg. Publisher of an extensive **Application Note (ANP)** series covering capacitor, inductor, EMI, and power-electronics characterization. Distinctive for being a smaller-than-Tier-1 MLCC vendor that publishes more analytical depth in its app notes than the Tier-1 vendors.

For this wiki: the **ANP114** by [[rene-kalbitz]] is the most directly useful piece — a SPICE-friendly analytic polarization model for Class II MLCCs.
