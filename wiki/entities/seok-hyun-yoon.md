# Seok-Hyun Yoon

Samsung Electro-Mechanics research lead in MLCC dielectric materials and reliability. Corresponding author on multiple foundational SEMCO MLCC papers in the wiki, including:

- [[chun-2024-screp-batio3-mlcc-degradation|Chun et al. 2024]] on thermal activation energy of MLCC electrical degradation
- [[yoon-kim-2020-batio3-tetragonality-dc-bias|Yoon, Kim, Kim 2020]] on tetragonality and DC-bias characteristics

The Yoon group at SEMCO contributes much of the publicly-disclosed academic literature on commercial-grade thin-layer X7R MLCC reliability and structure-property relationships.
