# Evgeniy Dul'kin

Department of Applied Physics, [[hebrew-university-jerusalem|The Hebrew University of Jerusalem]]. First author of [[dulkin-roth-2020-batio3-curie-shift|Dul'kin & Roth 2020]], the literature-comparison paper establishing the equivalence of uniaxial pressure and electric field in shifting the BaTiO₃ Curie temperature.
