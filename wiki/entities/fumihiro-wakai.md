# Fumihiro Wakai

Sintering-mechanics theorist at the **Tokyo Institute of Technology**, known for a continuum framework describing the **driving force of sintering** in terms of the surface-energy / sintering-stress tensor of a porous body (cf. [[skorohod-olevsky-viscous-sintering]]). Senior author on [[okuma-2021-mlcc-electrode-nanoct|Okuma et al. 2021]], where his anisotropic-sintering framework underpins the interpretation of in-plane shrinkage anisotropy and Ni-electrode break-up observed by nano-CT.
