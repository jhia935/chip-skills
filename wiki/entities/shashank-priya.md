# Shashank Priya

Professor and Associate Vice President for Research at Penn State (formerly Virginia Tech). Major figure in **multifunctional ferroelectrics, energy harvesting, and dielectric composites** — broad program spanning bulk ceramics, MLCCs, and thin-film devices.

Last author on [[song-2017-compositionally-graded-mlcc|Song et al. 2017]] (*Scientific Reports*), the compositionally-graded MLCC paper that produced one of the most explicitly cross-characterized academic measurements of dielectric response across **both temperature and DC bias** for the same MLCC structure.
