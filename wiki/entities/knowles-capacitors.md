# Knowles Capacitors

Capacitor manufacturer, formerly part of Knowles Corporation (audio components). Knowles Capacitors specializes in **high-reliability MLCCs for aerospace, defense, medical, and high-voltage applications** — Class I C0G and Class II X7R parts in higher voltage classes than the typical consumer MLCC.

Notable application-engineering blog presence — see [[victor-lu|Victor Lu]]'s practitioner writeups on DC-bias, aging, and reliability ([[lu-knowles-2023-dc-bias-explained|"DC Bias Phenomenon Explained"]]).
